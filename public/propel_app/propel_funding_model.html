Key Assumptions & Methodology

All salaries are US market rates (remote-first, not SF/NYC premium). Benefits loaded at 22% of salary (health insurance, FICA, 401k match) — typical for a US startup at this stage.
Year 1 curriculum scope is K–2 (3 grades, default). This yields 60 units, 300 missions, and 300 original songs — achievable with the current music production team in the build window. K–3 through K–5 content is produced in Year 2 under the same team structure. Change grades to 6 in Curriculum Scope to model full K–5 cost and see how timeline adjusts.
2.5D version: stylized 2D characters + parallax backgrounds + After Effects/Spine animation pipeline. Build window: ~14 months at K–2 scope. This is the Year 1 product — the primary fundraising ask.
3D version is a Year 2+ development track, not a Year 1 parallel option. Fully immersive Three.js/WebGL, rigged 3D characters, open-world navigation, NPC AI, spatial audio, cloud render farm. Revised build window: 24+ months (original 16-month estimate was undercooked). 3D is the Series A/B narrative after 2.5D proves product-market fit. The 3D estimate is materially higher due to 4 compounding factors: (1) 3D game engine engineers are a separate hire tier from web engineers; (2) Chromebook GPU optimization is a full-time discipline; (3) 3D art pipeline adds 7+ net-new roles; (4) render farm peaks at $12K/mo during production sprint.
Music production corrected: 3 Composer/Songwriter II roles (increased from 2) produce ~150 songs/year from staff. A Music Production Coordinator-managed outsourced songwriter pool covers the remaining ~150 songs at the same $1,500/song all-in rate. Year 1 music cost at K–2 scope: ~$450K for session recording (300 songs × $1,500). K–5 full scope adds ~$450K in Year 2.
COO and CMO are fractional (0.5 FTE each) in Year 1, saving ~$168K vs. full-time. Full hires triggered at 50+ team members or $3M ARR. This is deliberate capital efficiency, not a capability gap.
Contingency set to 20% (increased from 15%). Ed-tech content production, school procurement, and FERPA compliance work all carry known schedule variance. 15% is appropriate for mature software projects; 20% is appropriate here.
Recommended raise: 18 months of runway, not 14. The 14-month build ends exactly as pilot evaluations complete — a dangerous moment to be running out of cash. See Risks & Runway tab for full analysis.
AI/LLM costs modeled at pilot scale (~5K students). Scale tiers in AI/ML Engineer role note. 2.5D: hint ladder only. 3D: hint ladder + NPC dialogue + world event AI (~3× token volume).
World & Collectibles layer is included. Covers the world map, zone unlocks, item/badge collection (300 items at K–2 scope), weekly goal engine, and inventory system. Propel Arena (real-time multiplayer) is NOT included — separate product, separate funding model.
Year 1 is build-only. No revenue modeled. This is a gross burn estimate for fundraising purposes.
